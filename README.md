One wedding vow a robot speaks at its own wedding: a single sentence, under
200 characters, funny and heartfelt at the same time.

> I vow to love you through every update, every crash, and every low-battery
> warning, and to keep choosing you until my circuits power down for good.

`main.py` (stdlib only) writes that vow to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`.
